Read `CLAUDE.md` at the repo root first. It is the canonical guide (apps, identification rules,
commands, non-negotiables), and `MANIFESTO.md` is the product contract every change must pass.

## Learned Workspace Facts

- Product and planning docs are organized under `docs/<app>/{strategy,design,engineering,gtm}`.
- Cross-app strategy and engineering conventions live under `docs/portfolio/`.
- Each app keeps its iOS project in `apps/<app>/ios/`, a future Android project in
  `apps/<app>/android/`, and the rules both must agree on in `apps/<app>/spec/`.
