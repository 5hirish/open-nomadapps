Built for coach engagement

The biggest button, the narratives' closing questions and the paywall all point at the AI coach, not at an answer.

Everything shown, nothing connected

Around twenty metrics, and no single "on track or not, and why".

Precision theatre

Unreliable estimates look exact and get badges; verdicts like "Poor" and "Out of range" have no stated basis.

Empty states that end the road

"No data" is never followed by a next step.

What Yukta does instead

Google HealthYukta
Weight, burned and intake on separate cards (1)One weekly answer: trend rate against your goal, intake against target, expenditure learned from your own scale and food log
Watch calories as an exact number with "Goal met" badges (2)No watch "calories burned". Expenditure always as a range, in kcal, formatted for the user's region, never celebrated
"No data" dead ends (3)Every empty state has one action: "No intake yet. Quick add, or connect a food logger."
"Out of range" with no range; REM judged (8, 9)Every verdict shows its target ("40 m under your 7 h target"); sleep stages are never graded
The best insight was bedtime (G2)Bedtime joins the sleep lever in v1: "Bedtime about 2 h later than your target", not just hours slept
Floating coach, two AI entry points, closing questions (6, 14, 17)AI is one inline card, off by default, never floating, never ending in a question
"Poor", "Goal not met", "X kg above goal" (5, 16)Descriptive words ("short night", "behind your pace"); weight as trend and rate, never "above goal"
"Gives your joints time to rebuild" (15)Every "Why it matters" claim names its study and evidence strength, and links to the evidence register
