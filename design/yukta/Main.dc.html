4 weeks ago {{targetLegend}} Last night

Why it matters

In a 14-day calorie deficit, people given 5.5 hours of sleep lost about the same weight as with 8.5 hours, but far less of it was fat: the fat share fell by 55%.

Moderate: a small trial Nedeltcheva et al., 2010

After short sleep, hunger rose about 24%, with ghrelin (the hunger hormone) up and leptin (the fullness hormone) down. Yukta can't measure hormones; sleep is the lever that moves them.

Moderate: a small trial Spiegel et al., 2004
