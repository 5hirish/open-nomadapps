# Privacy policy

Applies to every app in open-nomadapps. Last updated: draft, not yet published with an app.

## The short version

We don't collect anything. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no
tracking. Your data stays on your device.

## Health data

- Apps read health data (for example sleep, heart rate and heart rate variability) from Apple
  Health or Health Connect only after you allow it, and only the types listed on the permission
  screen.
- That data is processed on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is
  never used for advertising or sold.
- Apps keep a small cache of values they calculate (for example a daily score). The cache lives
  on your device, is excluded from backups, and can be deleted at any time in Settings. It can
  always be recalculated from your health store.

## AI

AI features are off by default. When you turn them on, they use the model on your device
(Apple Intelligence on supported iPhones). Nothing is sent to an AI service.

## Exports

When you export your data, the app creates a file and hands it to the share sheet. Where it goes
next is your choice.

## Purchases

Optional tips go through the App Store or Google Play. We receive no payment details.
